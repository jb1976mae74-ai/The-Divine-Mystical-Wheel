# GEMINI.md - Custom Project Instructions

## App Persona & Instructions
- **Project Name:** The Great Wheel of Mysteries
- **Domain:** Esoteric ciphers, ancient historical records, symbolism, and AI search capabilities.
- **Architecture:** Full-stack React + Express + Vite + `@google/genai` (Server-side Gemini proxy).
- **Design System:** High-contrast mystic & dark aesthetic, clean typography, responsive layout.
- **Environment & Port:** Always run Express on port 3000 (`0.0.0.0`). Keep secrets server-side.
